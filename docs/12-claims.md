# Helpful Water claims register

Every Helpful Water product reduces risk; none is described as making water safe. This register sets what each solution may say now, what it may say only after accredited testing, and what it must never say. It applies to the repository, packaging, instructions, talks and fundraising material alike. Method: STANDARDS section 25.5.

Table 1. Claims by solution.

| Solution | May say now (design intent, TRL 3) | Only after accredited, independent testing | Never say | Exclusions shown on the product |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| A straw | "Designed to reduce bacteria and protozoa in clear freshwater"; "carbon stage intended to improve taste and odour" | A measured log reduction for named bacteria and cyst surrogates, at stated flow, turbidity and life; a taste or chlorine reduction under NSF/ANSI 42 | "Purifies", "sterilises", "virus-proof", "makes any water safe", "removes all contaminants" | Viruses, salt, arsenic, nitrate, fluoride, lead, PFAS, pesticides, fuel and industrial chemicals; do not freeze when wet |
| B bottle cartridge | As A, for a bottle | As A | As A | As A |
| C gravity | "Designed to reduce bacteria and protozoa in household water for drinking and cooking" | Log reductions by class against the WHO household treatment scheme, at the stated flow and life | As A; no claim that it serves infants unless tested at the infant-use volume and paired with D where viruses are a risk | As A |
| D virus add-on | "Doses chlorine at the WHO-recommended household rate to address viruses and bacteria when used with a filter" | A measured free chlorine residual and contact time at stated pH, temperature and turbidity; virus log reduction for the combined system | "Kills everything", "makes water safe"; any claim against *Cryptosporidium* from chlorine alone | Chlorine does not inactivate *Cryptosporidium*; wait the full contact time; use a filter for cysts |

## Findings carried from the solution STEM reviews (2026-11-11)

- *Leptospira* (about 0.1 um across) is excluded from every membrane claim unless it is in the accredited challenge (HWA-STM-001).
- A membrane is claimed only from an absolute rating with a bacterial challenge (for example *Brevundimonas diminuta*), never from its pore label.
- Where D is used with A, B or C, the chlorine dose and its full contact time come before the water reaches any carbon; carbon strips chlorine in seconds, so filtered water carries no residual and is not claimed as protected in storage (HWB-STM-001, HWD-STM-001).

## Required on-product warning (all solutions A to C)

"Reduces bacteria and protozoa. Does not remove viruses. Where viral disease is a risk, also treat the water with chlorine (solution D or a WHO-recommended tablet)." Shown as a pictogram (virus crossed out, with a tablet beside it) as well as in words, as the concept validation questions recommend.

## Release limitation

No prototype is used to treat water for drinking until pathogen performance, materials safety, integrity, source suitability and claims are validated by qualified laboratories. EPA public water system limits do not certify a consumer device.

# Helpful Water shared user requirements

What the people who use or buy Helpful Water products need, written from their side and shared by all four solutions. Each solution's own user requirements (`solutions/*/docs/03a-user-requirements.md`) repeat the rows that apply to it with the same IDs, so its product requirements and traceability matrix trace to this list. Sources: the personas, persona-driven design questions and use cases in the concept validation questions (2026), the Helpful Water update (2026-02-26) and the Rev A component specification (2026-10-14). Method: STANDARDS section 22.

## Use cases

1. **Disaster or flood (short term).** Mesh reduces grit, the membrane reduces bacteria and protozoa, and backflush keeps it usable; viruses and dissolved chemicals from runoff are not addressed (SC6 excluded as a sole barrier).
2. **Rural household with an unimproved source.** Consistent bacteria and protozoa reduction without power; not the right product where arsenic, fluoride or salt is the main risk.
3. **Camp or displacement setting.** Water from distribution points is recontaminated in storage; point-of-use treatment reduces risk at the time of drinking.
4. **Distrusted or unpalatable treated water.** Carbon improves taste; the membrane adds a layer against recontamination.
5. **Field teams.** A portable backup when supplies are inconsistent.

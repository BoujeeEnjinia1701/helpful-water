# Helpful Water program plan

Purpose: deliver clean, reliable and simple access to water for people without safe drinking water (Helpful Water update, 2026-02-26), through four open point-of-use solutions taken to a constructable design on paper (TRL 3, stage P3, the portfolio cap). The next gate, P4 (functional prototypes and accredited laboratory testing), stays closed until Amish opens it with Helpful Engineering. Amish settled the concept choice and every open decision in each solution's register on 2026-11-11.

## Kill criteria

From the Helpful Water update (2026-02-26), applied to each solution: stop or pause if consistent bacteria and protozoa reduction cannot be shown under realistic conditions; if flow becomes unusable or is not recoverable by backflush; if seal or membrane integrity cannot be kept in field handling; if total cost or complexity is not viable at humanitarian scale; or if partners decline because of safety, liability or usability concerns.

## Targets and key performance indicators

Table 2. Service-level targets (proposed in the viability shortlist for the copper straw and the bottle cartridge, carried here as program targets; adopted by Amish on 2026-11-11).

| Category | Target | Basis |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Functional prototype | 6 weeks from P4 opening | Viability shortlist |
| Bacteria and protozoa reduction | At least 99.9 % (3 log) as a first gate; the Rev A prototype goal is 6 log bacteria and 3 log cysts | Viability shortlist; Rev A specification section 7 |
| Flow | 300 to 500 mL/min under average suction (shortlist); at least 200 mL/min at 10 kPa or less (Rev A) | Two sources; reconciled per solution in PRS |
| Durability | At least 90 days of daily use | Viability shortlist |
| Unit cost at mass production | USD 1 (straw, shortlist), under USD 8 (bottle cartridge, shortlist) | Value-engineering targets, not limits |
| Mass and packing | Straw 100 g or less | Viability shortlist |
| User testing | 80 % positive among pilot participants; 90 % follow the cleaning sequence unassisted | Shortlist; Rev A |
| Leaching | Copper below 1.3 mg/L where any copper is present (copper is not in the design) | Shortlist |

Table 3. Key performance indicators.

| Category | KPI | Measurement |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Efficacy | Reduction of E. coli and *Giardia* or *Cryptosporidium* surrogates | Log reduction, pre against post |
| Flow | Mean flow against target | mL/min at stated pressure |
| Durability | Volume before 50 % flow loss; backflush recovery | Litres; percent |
| Adoption | Correct use and sustained use at 3, 6, 12 months | Survey and observation percent |
| Taste | Taste complaints | Percent of users |
| Cost | Cost per litre over life; cost per person per year | USD |
| Logistics | Units per cubic metre shipped | Count |
| Sustainability | Recyclable or biodegradable share | Percent of mass |
| Health impact (for funders) | Diarrhoeal incidence attributable to the device, designed into deployment | Partner study |

## Support needed from Helpful Engineering

- Design: CAD engineers to validate specifications, dimensions and feasibility (now through this repository).
- Testing and research: lab protocols and partners for microbial testing and turbidity and flow benchmarking.
- External partners to explore: university water microbiology laboratories, fabrication partners for potting, housings and sealing, and humanitarian and public health partners for field usability.

## 60 to 90 day plan once P4 opens (Rev A specification section 10)

Table 7. Gated plan.

| Window | Outputs and decision |
| --- | --- |
| Days 0 to 15 | Confirm target sources, requirements and claims; membrane supplier shortlist; freedom-to-operate review |
| Days 16 to 35 | Prototype A and B options with qualified membrane modules; hydraulic mapping and leak tests with non-consumable test water |
| Days 36 to 60 | Optimise mesh, fibre and carbon; backflush and integrity under abuse; begin accredited pathogen testing; human-factors trials with known potable water only |
| Days 61 to 90 | Review laboratory results and costs; updated BOM, maintenance procedure and risk register; go, pivot or stop recommendation to sponsors |

# Helpful Water market and viability

The first buyers are NGOs and humanitarian logistics networks, with consumer and emergency-kit sales as a possible second channel that funds humanitarian distribution, as LifeStraw did (concept validation questions, 2026). Procurement compares products on cost per litre over the device life and on independent test evidence, so both are designed in from the start. Prices were checked 2026-11-11.

## Viability metrics

- Cost per litre over the device life, including every consumable (carbon, chlorine) and its resupply.
- Cost per person per year (the shortlist target for the bottle cartridge was under USD 10 per user per year).
- Correct and sustained use at 3, 6 and 12 months, with an acceptance threshold set with the partner (the Kenya figures of 24 % non-use and 56 % correct use at 12 months are the warning).

## Open route

Open drawings under CERN-OHL-P-2.0 let regional moulders and assemblers make the housings; qualified membrane modules and carbon are bought from suppliers with data sheets and lot traceability. Helpful Engineering's volunteer network and WASH partners carry the designs to pilots once accredited tests are done.

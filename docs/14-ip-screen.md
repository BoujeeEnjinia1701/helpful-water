# Helpful Water patent and freedom-to-operate screen

A first desk screen, not legal advice. Country-specific freedom-to-operate opinions from a patent attorney come before any product is made for distribution, as the Rev A specification requires. Open hardware publication does not clear a third party's patent. Checked 2026-11-11.

# Helpful Water regulatory and standards register

The standards that shape claims, materials and testing for all four solutions, researched once for the program and cited by each solution. Not legal advice; anything marked "to assess" needs a qualified regulatory adviser before any product claim. Sources checked 2026-11-11.

Table 1. Regulatory and standards register.

| # | Standard or regulation | Jurisdiction | What it covers | Applicability | Owner (role) | Status |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| W1 | WHO International Scheme to Evaluate Household Water Treatment Technologies | International | Star ratings by log reduction of bacteria, viruses and protozoa | Humanitarian procurement reference for C, and A or B with D | Program lead | Applies as a target (HWP-CLM-001) |
| W2 | WHO Guidelines for drinking-water quality | International | E. coli not detectable in 100 mL; chemical guideline values, including chlorine 5 mg/L | Verification point; D dose ceiling | Microbiology lead | Applies |
| W3 | EPA Guide Standard and Protocol for Testing Microbiological Water Purifiers (portable purifier reference) | United States (reference) | 6 log bacteria, 4 log viruses, 3 log cysts for a purifier claim | Only for a combined system with D | Microbiology lead | Not claimed at TRL 3 |
| W4 | NSF Protocol P231 | United States (voluntary) | Microbiological purifier testing | Certification path after D is paired | Regulatory specialist | To assess |
| W5 | NSF/ANSI 42 | United States (voluntary) | Aesthetic effects: taste, odour, chlorine | Carbon stages | Regulatory specialist | Target |
| W6 | NSF/ANSI 53 and 401 | United States (voluntary) | Health contaminants; emerging contaminants | Not claimed | Regulatory specialist | Does not apply |
| W7 | NSF/ANSI/CAN 61 and 372 | United States and Canada (voluntary) | Health effects of drinking-water contact materials; lead content | Every wetted part | Materials specialist | Design rule |
| W8 | US EPA National Primary Drinking Water Regulations, Lead and Copper Rule, Secondary Standards | United States | Public water systems | Source screening benchmarks only; they do not certify a consumer device | Program lead | Reference only |
| W9 | Pesticide or biocide registration for disinfectant claims (for example US FIFRA; EU Biocidal Products Regulation) | United States, European Union, others | Products that claim to kill microbes, including chlorine tablets and silver-impregnated media | D, and any silver or antimicrobial media in A to C | Regulatory specialist | To assess before any disinfection claim |
| W10 | EU Drinking Water Directive (EU) 2020/2184, materials in contact with drinking water | European Union | Hygiene requirements for contact materials | Materials, if sold in the EU | Materials specialist | To assess |
| W11 | National registration in target countries | Partner countries | Water treatment product approval, import and labelling | All solutions before distribution | Partner organisation | To assess per country |
| W12 | ISO 7010 and ISO 3864 safety signs | International | Pictograms and safety colours | On-product pictograms | Industrial designer | Applies as guidance |

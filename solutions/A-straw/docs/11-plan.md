# Helpful Water A: filter straw project plan

Solution A is at stage P3 and TRL 3 on paper: a constructable design with a build plan. The next gate, P4 (prototype and accredited laboratory testing), stays closed until Amish opens it with Helpful Engineering (HWP-PLN-001). Amish decided the concept (D1), the membrane grade rule (D2), the flow target (D3) and D4 to D12 on 2026-11-11 (HWA-DEC-001); no decision is open. Method: STANDARDS sections 19, 23 and 25.7.

## Purpose

Reduce exposure to waterborne bacteria and protozoa for one person drinking from clear freshwater, stored water or distrusted tap water, without power, at a humanitarian cost per litre (HWP-PLN-001 purpose, applied to a straw).

## Kill criteria

From the Helpful Water update (2026-02-26), carried from HWP-PLN-001 and applied to the straw. Pause or stop solution A if:

1. it cannot show consistent bacteria and protozoa reduction under realistic conditions (PR-001, PR-006);
2. flow becomes unusable or is not recovered by backflush (PR-002, PR-003, PR-026), including if no membrane grade gives both the log reductions and at least the PR-003 child flow;
3. seal or membrane integrity cannot be kept in field handling (PR-007, PR-012, PR-016);
4. total cost or complexity is not viable at humanitarian scale (PR-020);
5. partners decline because of safety, liability or usability concerns.

## Targets and key performance indicators

The program targets of HWP-PLN-001 Table 2 apply. For the straw: flow 200 mL/min at 10 kPa (Rev A, PR-002) with 300 to 500 mL/min under average suction from the shortlist recorded as not reachable (HWA-CAL-001 section 4); 3 log bacteria and protozoa as the first gate and the Rev A goal of 6 log bacteria and 3 log cysts (PR-001, PR-006); at least 90 days of daily use; unit cost USD 1 at mass production from the shortlist against USD 1 to 5 in this design's value engineering; 100 g or less (66 g); 90 % of testers follow the cleaning sequence unassisted. KPIs as HWP-PLN-001 Table 3.

## 60 to 90 day plan once P4 opens

As HWP-PLN-001 Table 7. For the straw: days 0 to 15, Amish's decisions D1 to D3, RFQ and attorney; days 16 to 35, ten prototypes and the first checks with known potable test water; days 36 to 60, fouling and backflush (TE), integrity after drops and freezing (TF), air-hold check validation, accredited pathogen tests begin (TB); days 61 to 90, results, BOM and go, pivot or stop against the kill criteria.

A membrane engineer to review HWA-CAL-001 and the module drawing; introductions to straw-core makers and to a water microbiology laboratory; a field partner to answer the carbon resupply question.

# Helpful Water A: filter straw regulatory and standards register

The program register HWP-REG-001 (items W1 to W12) applies to the straw as written there and is not repeated; this register states how each applies to solution A and adds what is specific to a straw. Not legal advice; anything marked "to assess" needs a qualified regulatory adviser before any product claim. Checked 2026-11-11. Method: STANDARDS section 23.

Table 1. Regulatory and standards register for solution A.

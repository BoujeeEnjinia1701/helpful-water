# Helpful Water A: filter straw user requirements

What users of a personal filter straw need, written from their side. Rows UR-001 to UR-022 are those of the shared list HWP-URS-001 that apply to solution A, with the same IDs and wording; UR-013 (household volume) belongs to solution C and is left out. UR-101 and UR-102 are specific to the straw. Product requirements are in HWA-PRS-001 (`03-requirements.md`) and the links in HWA-RTM-001 (`03b-traceability.md`). Method: STANDARDS section 22.

Table 1. User requirements.

| ID | Category | Title | Need | Priority | Source |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| UR-001 | Performance | Bacteria | Reduce the bacteria in water from the source classes the solution is designed for | High | All personas; NGO buyers (HWP-URS-001) |
| UR-002 | Performance | Protozoa | Reduce protozoan cysts, including *Cryptosporidium* and *Giardia* | High | All personas; NGO buyers |
| UR-003 | Performance | Viruses addressed | Have a way to address viruses where viral disease is a risk, without implying the filter alone does it | High | NGO buyers; immunocompromised user |
| UR-004 | Usability | Usable flow | Get enough water at the suction a child or elderly user can give | High | Child; elderly user |
| UR-005 | Usability | Ready to use | Use it straight from the pack with no power, assembly or tools | High | Crisis recipient; field worker |
| UR-006 | Usability | Simple instructions | Understand use, cleaning, limits and end of life from pictograms in three steps or fewer | High | Crisis recipient; low literacy users |
| UR-007 | Maintenance | Low daily upkeep | Keep it working with under 2 minutes of upkeep a day and no tools | High | Crisis recipient; elderly user |
| UR-008 | Safety | Know when it fails | Know when the device is clogged, spent or damaged, so it never fails silently | High | All; field worker |
| UR-009 | Safety | No bypass | Service it (clean, change a cartridge) without letting dirty water reach the clean side | High | Low dexterity user; field worker |
| UR-010 | Safety | Hygienic storage | Store it wet or dry in a hot climate without it growing germs | High | All |
| UR-011 | Safety | Safe materials | Nothing in it adds harmful substances to the water | High | All; NGO buyers |
| UR-012 | Deployment and assembly | Portable | Carry it in a pocket or kit (under 25 cm long and 3 cm across for personal devices) | Medium | Traveller; field worker |
| UR-014 | Cost | Affordable | Cost per person per year, and per litre over the device life, that NGO buyers can justify against tablets and sealed filters | High | NGO buyers |
| UR-015 | Logistics | Resupply | Get any consumable (carbon, chlorine) where the device is used, or have none | Medium | Field worker; NGO buyers |
| UR-016 | Environment and durability | Shelf life and transit | Survive warehouse heat, transit and storage before first use | Medium | NGO buyers |
| UR-017 | Environment and durability | Drops and freezing | Survive drops, and warn or resist where freezing can occur | Medium | Traveller; highland users |
| UR-018 | Usability | Taste | Accept the taste and smell, so people keep using it | Medium | Elderly user; tap-water users |
| UR-019 | Maintenance | Cleanable prefilter | Rinse the coarse prefilter in the field | Medium | High-turbidity user |
| UR-020 | Safety | Shared use | Share the device in a household without passing infection mouth to mouth | Medium | Household carer |
| UR-021 | Environment and durability | End of life | Dispose of spent parts without waste infrastructure | Low | NGO buyers; communities |
| UR-022 | Deployment and assembly | Regional manufacture | Have parts made or assembled regionally | Low | NGO buyers; Helpful Engineering |
| UR-101 | Maintenance | Restore flow | Bring back the flow after drinking turbid water by flushing it with clean water, without the dirt or the flush reaching the carbon or the mouthpiece | High | High-turbidity user; field worker (Rev A item 10) |
| UR-102 | Logistics | Light | Carry it with little weight: 100 g or less | Low | Traveller; field worker (viability shortlist) |

Categories: deployment and assembly, performance, usability, safety, environment and durability, logistics, maintenance, cost, regulatory. Priorities: High, Medium, Low.

# Helpful Water A: filter straw market and viability

The straw competes with LifeStraw Personal and the Sawyer Mini for emergency kits and individual use, where it offers a rinseable mesh, a backflush that never passes the carbon, a field integrity check and an open design for regional moulding; against chlorine tablets it costs less per litre at scale but protects only the person drinking and does not cover viruses. The program market, buyers and metrics are in HWP-MKT-001. Prices checked 2026-11-11. Method: STANDARDS section 23.

At about 2 L of drinking water a day one straw lasts about 2.7 years at 2,000 L, so the cost per person per year at scale is about USD 1.3 with the carbon refills (USD 0.0017 a litre x 730 L); that is within the under USD 10 per user per year the shortlist set for the bottle cartridge (HWP-MKT-001).

## Open route

The housing, caps, cartridge and mouthpiece are open drawings under CERN-OHL-P-2.0 that a regional moulder can make in polypropylene from two or three simple tools; makerspaces can print them for pilots. The membrane module is bought from qualified makers with certificates and lot traceability, and the carbon from water-treatment media suppliers. Helpful Engineering's volunteer network and WASH partners carry the design to pilots once accredited tests are done; no pilot uses an untested straw.

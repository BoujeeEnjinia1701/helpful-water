# Helpful Water A: filter straw feasibility assessment

Option A1 is feasible to build and to make cheaply; whether it is worth deploying turns on two things a laboratory must show: enough flow at the membrane grade that also gives the bacteria and cyst log reductions, and an integrity check that catches a broken fibre. Method: STANDARDS section 23.

Table 1. Feasibility by aspect.

| Aspect | Assessment | Rating | Risks and notes | Evidence |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Design | Within known science after fixes F1 to F11; flow 110 to 179 mL/min at 10 kPa (Rev A's 200 not met on paper), below the 400 mL/min users want | Medium | Membrane grade (D2) and flow target (D3) open | HWA-STM-001, HWA-CAL-001 |
| Materials | Every wetted class exists in listed grades; printed prototypes are not listed | High | Carbon regrowth; potting leachables (supplier) | HWA-REG-001 A7, A13 |
| Manufacturing and assembly | Printed PP parts, cast silicone, one bought module; no potting by the builder; moulding route clear | High | Module to the program's drawing needs a willing supplier (C1) | HWA-BLD-001, HWA-DDR-002 |
| Power and controls | None: suction only | High | None | Not relevant |
| Integrity | Two O-rings in series and a factory potting; air-hold check proposed | Medium | Silent fibre damage remains the top hazard; check unproven (S20) | HWA-RSK-001 |
| Supply of parts | Module, mesh, O-rings and GAC from named supplier types | Medium | No quote yet for a 21.8 mm collar module | `bom/bom.csv` |
| Cost | USD 17.46 prototype; about USD 0.002 a litre at scale | High | Module price at volume to assess | HWA-CAL-001 section 10 |

Ratings: High, Medium, Low.

# Helpful Water D: chlorine add-on for viruses design decisions register

Six decisions are open and wait for Amish (D1, D3 to D7); the most consequential for the program is D3 (no chlorine residual survives the carbon of A to C). D2, the independent STEM review, was run on 2026-11-11 and is closed. Twelve detail fixes were made under Amish's standing instruction of 2026-10-23 (STANDARDS section 20 fix rule), six of them after the independent review, and can be reversed by him. Method: STANDARDS section 18.

## Value engineering

Value-engineering target: USD 10. Estimated cost of the constructable design: USD 9.15 (USD 0.85 under the target). Main cost drivers: the printed cap body (USD 2.11) and the twenty test tablets at retail (USD 3.20). Savings worth trying: buy test tablets at humanitarian prices through a partner (about USD 0.01 each); print the inserts, hub and end caps in one job; a moulded scale version is estimated at USD 1.40.

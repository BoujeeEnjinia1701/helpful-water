# Helpful Water D: concept selection

**Status: Decided by Amish on 2026-11-11 (recommendation adopted)** (HWD-DEC-001 D1).

## Context

Solution D is the chlorine co-treatment that pairs with A, B or C to address viruses (Amish, 2026-11-11: "No virus claim, co-treat"). Three working principles were modelled to D3 and scored against HWD-URS-001 (HWD-OPT-001).

## Options

- D1 tablet dose cap with ball timer: 174 of 215.
- D2 metered liquid doser with ball timer: 143 of 215.
- D3 erosion feeder in the raw bucket: 85 of 215, and **Fails** on the STEM check (HWD-STM-001 X1, X2): a contact-time release cannot hold the dose between 1 and 5 mg/L when standing time varies from 30 minutes to a day. Dropped.

## Proposal

Carry D1. It gives a fixed dose from standard humanitarian tablets with a 5 year strip shelf life, cannot be overdosed by a wrong or extra tablet (keyed wells, two-tablet stop), shows the tablet dissolving and the wait without a watch, and fits B's 28 mm neck and the 28 mm port on C's bucket (HWD-DEC-001 D4). D2 is 17.8 % behind, outside the 10 % band; its weakness is the liquid's loss of strength with age and heat, which the user cannot see. Graft D2's best idea where a partner already distributes SWS liquid: offer the ball timer as a clip.

## Consequences

The drawing, BOM and build plan describe D1. Whole matched tablets are used; tablets are never split, since half-tablet doses are inaccurate and the broken face takes up moisture. If Amish chooses D2 the timer, labels and claims carry over; the cap body, inserts and plug do not.

# Helpful Water D: chlorine co-treatment add-on user requirements

What the people who use solution D need, written from their side. Rows UR-003 to UR-022 repeat the shared user requirements of HWP-URS-001 that list D, with the same IDs and priorities; UR-101 to UR-104 are specific to a chlorine co-treatment and come from the users in HWD-PRB-001 Table 1. Rows of HWP-URS-001 that do not list D (bacteria and protozoa reduction, flow, daily upkeep, bypass, hygienic storage, portability, household volume, drops and freezing, prefilter and shared use) belong to the filters A to C. Method: STANDARDS section 22.

Table 1. User requirements for solution D.

| ID | Category | Title | Need | Priority | Source |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| UR-003 | Performance | Viruses addressed | Have a way to address viruses where viral disease is a risk, without implying the filter alone does it | High | NGO buyers; immunocompromised user (HWP-URS-001) |
| UR-005 | Usability | Ready to use | Use it straight from the pack with no power, assembly or tools | High | Crisis recipient; field worker (HWP-URS-001) |
| UR-006 | Usability | Simple instructions | Understand use, limits and end of life from pictograms in three steps or fewer | High | Crisis recipient; low literacy users (HWP-URS-001) |
| UR-008 | Safety | Know when it fails | Know when the dose did not go in or the wait is not over, so it never fails silently | High | All; field worker (HWP-URS-001) |
| UR-011 | Safety | Safe materials | Nothing in it adds harmful substances to the water, including too much chlorine or its by-products | High | All; NGO buyers (HWP-URS-001) |
| UR-014 | Cost | Affordable | Cost per person per year and per litre that NGO buyers can justify against tablets alone | High | NGO buyers (HWP-URS-001) |
| UR-015 | Logistics | Resupply | Get the chlorine where the device is used | Medium | Field worker; NGO buyers (HWP-URS-001) |
| UR-016 | Environment and durability | Shelf life and transit | Survive warehouse heat, transit and storage before first use, and keep the chlorine strong | Medium | NGO buyers (HWP-URS-001) |
| UR-018 | Usability | Taste | Accept the taste and smell, so people keep using it | Medium | Elderly user; tap-water users (HWP-URS-001) |
| UR-021 | Environment and durability | End of life | Dispose of spent parts and foil without waste infrastructure | Low | NGO buyers; communities (HWP-URS-001) |
| UR-022 | Deployment and assembly | Regional manufacture | Have parts made or assembled regionally | Low | NGO buyers; Helpful Engineering (HWP-URS-001) |
| UR-101 | Safety | Right dose for the container | Put in the right amount of chlorine for my bottle or bucket without measuring, and never too much by mistake | High | Crisis recipient; household carer; child (HWD-PRB-001) |
| UR-102 | Usability | Know the wait is over | See when the water has had its full contact time, without a watch, including in cold water | High | Household carer; elderly user (HWD-PRB-001) |

Categories: deployment and assembly, performance, usability, safety, environment and durability, logistics, maintenance, cost, regulatory. Priorities: High, Medium, Low.

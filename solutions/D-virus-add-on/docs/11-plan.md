# Helpful Water D: chlorine co-treatment add-on project plan

D is at TRL 3 on paper (stage P3, the portfolio cap): a constructable dose cap with calculations, drawings, a priced BOM and a build plan. The next gate, P4 (a functional prototype checked with known potable test water and the TJ chlorine-dosing family), stays closed until Amish opens it with Helpful Engineering. Amish decided HWD-DEC-001 D1 to D7 on 2026-11-11, including D3 (C stays carbon-free) and D4 (the 28 mm port in C's lid); no decision is open.

## Purpose

Give households and field users of the A to C filters a simple, tool-free way to dose chlorine at the household rate and wait the full contact time before filtering, so that viruses are addressed where viral disease is a risk (HWP-PLN-001 purpose; Amish, 2026-11-11: "No virus claim, co-treat").

## Kill criteria

From HWP-PLN-001, applied to D, with D's own: stop or pause if the dose cannot be held between the household rate and the 5 mg/L guideline value in realistic use; if users cannot tell when the contact time is complete (TH); if the combined system cannot show virus reduction under realistic conditions (TB); if the consumable cannot be resupplied where the device is used; if total cost or complexity is not viable beside tablets alone; or if partners decline because of chemical safety, registration or liability concerns.

## Targets and key performance indicators

Table 1. Targets for D (Decided by Amish on 2026-11-11).

| Category | Target | Basis |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Dose | 2.0 mg/L (1.5 to 2.5), 4.0 for settled turbid water; never above 5 | WHO GV; IFRC; PR-001, PR-005 |
| Residual | At least 0.5 mg/L at 30 min (clear water) | WHO GDWQ; PR-002 |
| Virus reduction, combined system | Toward the WHO household scheme targets (3 log two-star, 5 log three-star), as targets only | HWP-CLM-001 Table 2 |
| Timer | At least 30 min at 25 C | PR-008 |
| Correct use | 90 % follow dose, wait, filter unaided | HWP-PLN-001 Table 2 (cleaning sequence target, applied to D) |
| Cost | Under USD 10 prototype; about USD 1.40 at scale | PR-019 |

Table 2. Key performance indicators (HWP-PLN-001 Table 3, for D).

| Category | KPI | Measurement |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Efficacy | Free chlorine at 30 and 60 min; virus log reduction of the combined system | mg/L (DPD); log10 (TB) |
| Usability | Share who wait the full time and filter after | Observation percent |
| Taste | Taste complaints after the filter | Percent of users |
| Cost | Cost per litre and per household a year | USD |
| Logistics | Tablet stock-outs at the point of use | Days per year |

## Minimum viable team

From HWP-PLN-001 Table 4: the water chemist (lead for D), a mechanical and design-for-manufacture engineer, the water microbiologist and accredited laboratory (TB virus protocol for the combined system), the materials and regulatory quality specialist, and the industrial designer and human-factors lead.

Table 6. Gated plan.

| --- | --- |
| Days 0 to 15 | Partner's tablet sizes and strengths (C1); C interface agreed (D4); residual decision (D3) |
| Days 16 to 35 | Print five caps; seal, key and timer checks; fluid grade tuned (C2) |
| Days 36 to 60 | TJ bench dosing with known potable test water at pH 6 to 8.5, 5 to 30 C, spiked turbidity; pictogram comprehension (TH) |

## Support needed from Helpful Engineering

A water chemist to own the dosing tests; introductions to a WASH partner that distributes NaDCC (tablet sizes, resupply, ministry approvals); a laboratory able to run a virus protocol on the combined systems.

## Next steps and decisions

D1 to D7 were decided by Amish on 2026-11-11 (HWD-DEC-001). The design looks ready for a TRL 4 build once the tablets are measured; that is a recommendation, not a start.

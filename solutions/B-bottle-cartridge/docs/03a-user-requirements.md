# Helpful Water B: bottle cartridge user requirements

What users of the bottle cartridge need, written from their side. Rows UR-001 to UR-022 repeat the shared program requirements of HWP-URS-001 that apply to solution B, with the same IDs and priorities (UR-013, household volume, belongs to solution C and is left out). UR-101 to UR-103 are specific to B. Product requirements are in HWB-PRS-001 and the links in HWB-RTM-001. Still a desk draft, to review with users through a field partner. Method: STANDARDS section 22.

Table 1. User requirements.

| ID | Category | Title | Need | Priority | Source |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| UR-001 | Performance | Bacteria | Reduce the bacteria in water from the source classes the solution is designed for | High | All personas; NGO buyers (HWP-URS-001) |
| UR-002 | Performance | Protozoa | Reduce protozoan cysts, including *Cryptosporidium* and *Giardia* | High | All personas; NGO buyers (HWP-URS-001) |
| UR-003 | Performance | Viruses addressed | Have a way to address viruses where viral disease is a risk, without implying the filter alone does it | High | NGO buyers; immunocompromised user (HWP-URS-001; B pairs with D) |
| UR-004 | Usability | Usable flow | Get enough water at the squeeze or effort a child or elderly user can give | High | Child; elderly user (HWP-URS-001) |
| UR-005 | Usability | Ready to use | Use it straight from the pack with no power, assembly or tools | High | Crisis recipient; field worker (HWP-URS-001) |
| UR-006 | Usability | Simple instructions | Understand use, cleaning, limits and end of life from pictograms in three steps or fewer | High | Crisis recipient; low literacy users (HWP-URS-001) |
| UR-007 | Maintenance | Low daily upkeep | Keep it working with under 2 minutes of upkeep a day and no tools | High | Crisis recipient; elderly user (HWP-URS-001) |
| UR-008 | Safety | Know when it fails | Know when the device is clogged, spent or damaged, so it never fails silently | High | All; field worker (HWP-URS-001) |
| UR-009 | Safety | No bypass | Service it (clean, change a cartridge) without letting dirty water reach the clean side | High | Low dexterity user; field worker (HWP-URS-001) |
| UR-010 | Safety | Hygienic storage | Store it wet or dry in a hot climate without it growing germs | High | All (HWP-URS-001) |
| UR-011 | Safety | Safe materials | Nothing in it adds harmful substances to the water | High | All; NGO buyers (HWP-URS-001) |
| UR-012 | Deployment and assembly | Portable | Carry it in a pocket or kit (under 25 cm long and 3 cm across for personal devices) | Medium | Traveller; field worker (HWP-URS-001) |
| UR-014 | Cost | Affordable | Cost per person per year, and per litre over the device life, that NGO buyers can justify against tablets and sealed filters | High | NGO buyers (HWP-URS-001) |
| UR-015 | Logistics | Resupply | Get any consumable (carbon, chlorine) where the device is used, or have none | Medium | Field worker; NGO buyers (HWP-URS-001) |
| UR-016 | Environment and durability | Shelf life and transit | Survive warehouse heat, transit and storage before first use | Medium | NGO buyers (HWP-URS-001) |
| UR-017 | Environment and durability | Drops and freezing | Survive drops, and warn or resist where freezing can occur | Medium | Traveller; highland users (HWP-URS-001) |
| UR-018 | Usability | Taste | Accept the taste and smell, so people keep using it | Medium | Elderly user; tap-water users (HWP-URS-001) |
| UR-019 | Maintenance | Cleanable prefilter | Rinse the coarse prefilter in the field | Medium | High-turbidity user (HWP-URS-001) |
| UR-020 | Safety | Shared use | Share the device in a household without passing infection mouth to mouth | Medium | Household carer (HWP-URS-001) |
| UR-021 | Environment and durability | End of life | Dispose of spent parts without waste infrastructure | Low | NGO buyers; communities (HWP-URS-001) |
| UR-022 | Deployment and assembly | Regional manufacture | Have parts made or assembled regionally | Low | NGO buyers; Helpful Engineering (HWP-URS-001) |
| UR-101 | Deployment and assembly | Fits my bottle | Screw it onto the plastic water or soft-drink bottles that are already in the household or local shops | High | Field worker; household carer (viability shortlist item 9: "fits global bottle standards") |
| UR-102 | Usability | No-squeeze option | Get treated water without squeezing, for users with weak or painful hands | Medium | Elderly user; user with limited dexterity (HWB-PRB-001 Table 1) |
| UR-103 | Maintenance | Long life | Keep using one cartridge for 6 to 12 months, changing only the carbon | Medium | NGO buyers; households (viability shortlist item 9) |

Categories: deployment and assembly, performance, usability, safety, environment and durability, logistics, maintenance, cost, regulatory. Priorities: High, Medium, Low.

# Helpful Water B: bottle cartridge STEM check

Verdict: no claim fails. Of 33 claims, 14 sit within known science and 19 are plausible and stay open until supplier data or TRL 4 tests close them, so `stem_check` is **open**. The check was run before any design and found four details of the briefed options that do not work as described; each fix keeps the working principle and was made under Amish's standing instruction of 2026-10-23 (HWB-DEC-001 F1 to F4, HWB-DDR-003): the carbon moves upstream of the membrane (S18), a duckbill valve lets air back into a squeezed bottle (S19), the gravity stand gets a wider base (S20) and the inverted bottle gets a vent tube (S21). An independent review on 2026-11-11 (section below) refuted one row (S22, the clean-side hold-up), weakened 16 and raised 11 missed checks; it led to six new rows (S28 to S33), five more fixes under the same instruction (F5 to F9) and two proposals for Amish (D8 housing end wall, D9 rating temperature). The largest open item is still the membrane's clean-water permeability: every flow figure rests on an assumed 1,000 L/(m2 h bar) in 20 C water, which the module maker must confirm (HWB-DEC-001 C1); in 10 C water every flow is about 23 % lower (S31). Method: STANDARDS sections 20 and 25.6. Calculations: `docs/04-calcs/stem_check.py`, which reads the results of `docs/04-calcs/sizing.py` (HWB-CAL-001).

> **Release limitation.** No prototype is used to treat water for drinking until its pathogen performance, materials safety, integrity, source suitability and claims are validated by qualified laboratories (HWP-CLM-001). Every number here is a paper estimate.

## Fields not relevant

- Electromagnetism, optics and nuclear radiation: the device has no power, light source or radiation.
- Electrochemistry: no electrodes or metals in contact with water beyond the 316L mesh, which is passive (copper was removed from the program design, HWP-PRB-001).
- Ecology and agronomy: no living material is used; disposal is covered in HWB-RSK-001 and HWB-MKT-001.

## Independent review

**Method.** On 2026-11-11 a second agent (Claude Opus 5.5 through the `claude` command line, run as a separate process with read, shell and web tools and no access to this document, the decision records or the design reasoning) was given only `docs/04-calcs/stem_results.csv`, `stem_check.py`, `sizing.py` with the model's input values, `sizing_results.json` and the cited sources. It was asked to refute every "Within known science" and "Plausible" verdict, to default to weak or refuted when unsure, to recompute every number and unit and to look for missed STANDARDS 25.6 water checks. Its findings were then checked by hand before anything was changed.

**Counts.** Of the 27 rows then in the table: **10 hold, 16 weak, 1 refuted** (S22). It also raised 11 missed checks (N1 refuted, N2 to N10 weak, N11 holds). Two findings did not survive checking: the B2 stand tips about the edge of a continuous 230 mm ring, not between leg feet, so S20 holds at 17.2 deg; and the 15.5 mm skirt depth already includes the compressed 1.5 mm gasket (the deck sits 1.5 mm above the lip), so S24 holds. The review also showed that the summary of version 0.1 miscounted: the script gave 16 within and 11 plausible, not 18 and 9.

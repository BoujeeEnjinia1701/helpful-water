# Helpful Water B: concept selection

**Status: Proposed, awaiting Amish.** B1 (squeeze cartridge) scored 246 and B2 (gravity drip stand) 222 of 310 in HWB-OPT-001; B2 is 9.8 % below B1, inside the 10 % band of STANDARDS section 21, so both go to Amish without a pick. B3 (press cup, 209) is not recommended.

## Context

The brief for solution B asked for at least two genuinely different working principles: squeeze through a hollow-fibre cartridge, gravity drip from an inverted bottle, and a third principle the STEM check supports. All three passed the STEM check after four detail fixes (HWB-DEC-001 F1 to F4).

## Options

1. **B1 alone.** Cheapest (USD 18.35 of prototype parts), pocketable, one-squeeze air test; squeeze effort for children and elderly users is the weak point (160 mL/min at a weak child's 5.8 kPa, clean; half that fouled).
2. **B2 alone.** No effort, fills cups for a household, longest carbon contact; slower (about 6 minutes a litre), a tall stand to knock over, more parts, not portable.
3. **B1 with B2's stand as an accessory.** The same cartridge serves both: squeezed when carried, hung in a stand at home. The stand adds cost (estimate USD 8 to 10 of parts) and, with the standard module, gives about 10 minutes per litre clean.

## Consequences

The TRL 3 drawings, BOM and build plan describe B1, since B2's cartridge is the same stack turned over. If Amish picks B2, its longer module, vent tube, stand drawings, BOM lines and build steps are the next work; its model already exists at D3.

## Decision

None yet. Proposed, awaiting Amish (HWB-DEC-001 D1).

# Helpful Water B: bottle cartridge regulatory and standards register

The program register HWP-REG-001 (W1 to W12) applies to solution B in full and is cited, not copied; this register adds what is specific to a cartridge that screws onto other makers' bottles. Not legal advice; anything marked "to assess" needs a qualified reviewer before any product claim. Sources checked 2026-11-11.

# Helpful Water B: bottle cartridge market and viability

The bottle cartridge serves emergency kits, households, schools and camps that already have PET bottles, and competes with squeeze filters that sell for USD 17 to 46 and with chlorine tablets at about USD 0.02 a litre (HWP-MKT-001). An open design lets regional moulders make the housings while a qualified module and carbon are bought, which closed squeeze filters do not allow. Prices checked 2026-11-11. Method: STANDARDS section 23.

## Viability (HWB-CAL-001 section 7)

- Prototype parts USD 18.35 against the USD 20 value-engineering target; scale estimate USD 7.05 against the shortlist's under USD 8.
- Over a 9-month life at 2 L a day (547 L) with three extra carbon capsules: USD 8.10, which is **USD 0.015 a litre** and **USD 10.8 per user per year**, over the shortlist's USD 10 target; at a 12-month life about USD 8.5 per user per year. Tablets cost about USD 0.02 a litre but do not cover *Cryptosporidium*; the cartridge does not cover viruses, so the two are partners, not rivals.
- Correct and sustained use is the warning metric (HWP-MKT-001: 24 % non-use and 56 % correct use at 12 months in the Kenya deployment).

## Open route

Housings, collars, capsules and spouts are open under CERN-OHL-P-2.0 for regional printers and moulders; the module and carbon are bought from qualified suppliers with data sheets and lot traceability. Helpful Engineering's volunteer network and WASH partners carry the design to pilots once accredited tests are done (HWP-PLN-001).

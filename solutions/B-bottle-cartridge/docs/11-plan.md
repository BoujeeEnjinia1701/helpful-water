# Helpful Water B: bottle cartridge project plan

Solution B is at stage P3 and TRL 3 on paper: a constructable B1 design with drawings, a priced BOM and a build plan. The next gate, P4 (prototypes and accredited laboratory testing), stays closed until Amish opens it with Helpful Engineering. Decision requested now: the concept choice between B1 and B2 (HWB-DEC-001 D1) and the other open decisions D2 to D7. Method: STANDARDS sections 19, 23 and 25.7.

## Purpose

Deliver a low-cost, open, unpowered bottle cartridge designed to reduce bacteria and protozoa for families, schools and camps, used with chlorine where viruses are a risk (HWP-PLN-001 purpose, applied to B).

## Kill criteria

From HWP-PLN-001, applied to B: stop or pause if the cartridge cannot show consistent bacteria and protozoa reduction under realistic conditions; if flow at the persona efforts is unusable or is not recovered by backflush; if the header seal or membrane integrity cannot be kept through drops, carbon changes and storage; if the cost per user per year cannot approach USD 10 or the complexity is not viable at humanitarian scale; or if partners decline because of safety, liability or usability concerns.

## Targets and key performance indicators

Table 1. Targets that apply to B (HWP-PLN-001 Table 2; Proposed, awaiting Amish).

| Category | Target | B status on paper |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Bacteria and protozoa | At least 3 log as a first gate; 6 log bacteria and 3 log cysts as the goal | To test (PR-001, PR-002) |
| Flow | At least 200 mL/min at 10 kPa (Rev A); 300 to 500 mL/min at an average squeeze (shortlist) | 274 at 10 kPa and 465 at 17 kPa, clean, estimates |
| Durability | At least 90 days of daily use; B aims at 6 to 12 months | To test (PR-026) |
| Unit cost at scale | Under USD 8 (shortlist) | USD 7.05 (estimate) |
| Cost per user per year | Under USD 10 (shortlist) | USD 10.8 at 9 months; 8.5 at 12 months |
| Mass | About 100 g (shortlist) | About 118 g dry |
| User testing | 80 % positive; 90 % follow the cleaning sequence unassisted | To test (TH) |

KPIs are those of HWP-PLN-001 Table 3 (efficacy, flow, durability, adoption, taste, cost, logistics, sustainability).

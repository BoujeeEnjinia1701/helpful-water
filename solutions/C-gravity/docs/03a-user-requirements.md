# Helpful Water C: household gravity filter user requirements

What households need from solution C, written from their side. Rows UR-001 to UR-022 repeat the shared requirements of HWP-URS-001 that apply to C, with the same IDs and priorities; UR-012 (pocket size) and UR-020 (shared mouthpiece) do not apply to a household unit and are left out. UR-101 to UR-104 are specific to C and come from the users and stakeholders in HWC-PRB-001. These are a desk draft for review with households through a field partner (method: STANDARDS section 22).

## Use cases for C

1. **Rural household, unimproved source (SC2, SC3).** Fill through the cloth, settle, filter into the clean bucket; dose with D first where viral disease is a risk.
2. **Camp or displacement (SC5).** Water from a distribution point is chlorinated again with D in the raw bucket, filtered and kept in the closed clean bucket with a residual.
3. **Clinic or school point (secondary).** One unit serves several households in batches; the closed clean bucket replaces a shared open pot.

# Helpful Water C: STEM check fixes

**Status: decided under Amish's standing instruction of 2026-10-23 (STEM fix rule); Amish can reverse any of them.** Each fix keeps the working principle; none changes the pitch or the user-facing scope.

Table 1. Fixes.

| # | Row | What failed | Options weighed | Fix made | Cost |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| F1 | S18 | The briefed 420 mm frame tips at 14.2 deg with the raw bucket full and the clean bucket empty (criterion 15 deg) | Lower the top shelf (loses head); ballast (heavy); wider feet | 560 mm cross feet: 18.7 deg, 78 N at 1 m | About USD 1 of timber |
| F2 | S19 | With the module's orientation free, air pockets at the top of the shell and blocks fibre area; downflow in the hose stops it venting | Horizontal module (traps more air); a separate air vent valve; upright with a bleed screw | Module upright, raw inlet on top, bleed screw on the top cap (supplier request), opened at first fill | Within the module price (to confirm) |
| F3 | S20 | Two 20 L containers overflow when the clean one is not empty; a 5 L refill line still overflowed if the raw bucket was filled past its line | A float valve in the clean bucket (a wetted part on the clean side); a smaller raw batch; a lower refill line | 17 L fill line, and a refill line at 2.5 L, just above the tap, so even a brim-full raw batch (19.4 L) fits the 19.6 L free | Two marks |
| F4 | S21 | Nothing held the raw water for its 30 minutes of chlorine contact before filtering | Rely on instructions alone; a timer valve (complex) | Dosing port in the raw lid for D and a "shut 30 minutes after dosing" valve tag | USD 2.40 of printed parts |
| F5 | S22 | One 60 mL syringe stroke cannot pressurise a water-filled clean side to the 100 kPa air-test pressure (found by the independent review) | A hand pump with a gauge (cost, a second tool); a smaller test pressure (coarser test) | A luer check valve on the backflush port: several strokes, the firm 56 N push is the gauge | USD 0.80 |

The STEM check was rerun after each fix; rows S18 to S22 are closed by them (HWC-STM-001).

# Helpful Water C: household gravity filter STEM check

Verdict: no claim fails. Of 36 claims, 25 sit within known science and 11 are plausible and stay open until supplier data or TRL 4 tests close them, so `stem_check` is **open**. The check was run before any design and found four details of the briefed options that do not work as described, and the independent review found a fifth in the first design; each fix keeps the working principle and was made under Amish's standing instruction of 2026-10-23 (HWC-DEC-001 F1 to F5, HWC-DDR-003): the stand gets 560 mm feet because the briefed 420 mm frame tips at 14 deg when loaded (S18); the module stands upright with its raw inlet on top and a bleed screw so shell air can escape (S19); a fill line and a 2.5 L refill line keep even a brim-full batch from overflowing the clean bucket (S20); the lid gets a dosing port and the valve a 30 minute hold tag, so chlorine from solution D gets its contact time before filtering (S21); and a luer check valve lets the syringe reach the air-test pressure in several strokes (S22). No option's working principle fails, so C2 and C3 stay in the concept options; C2 misses the "few hours" target by physics (S31) and C3 reduces bacteria less (S35), which the scoring reflects. The largest open item is the membrane's permeability: every C1 flow figure rests on an assumed 1,000 L/(m2 h bar) (HWC-DEC-001 C1). Method: STANDARDS sections 20 and 25.6. Calculations: `docs/04-calcs/stem_check.py`, which reads the results of `docs/04-calcs/sizing.py` (HWC-CAL-001).

> **Release limitation.** No prototype is used to treat water for drinking until its pathogen performance, materials safety, integrity, source suitability and claims are validated by qualified laboratories (HWP-CLM-001). Every number here is a paper estimate.

## Verdict by option

- **C1 two-bucket hollow-fibre filter:** within known science; five details fixed (F1 to F5); open on the assumed permeability (S3), persona efforts (S7, S8), the module's chlorine tolerance (S13), residual in storage (S14), turbid-water backflush interval (S17), the module's pressure ratings (S26, S27), clean-side regrowth (S28) and materials (S29).

- **C3 plastic biosand filter:** within known science; about 1 to 2 log bacteria reduction in field data and up to 30 days of ripening (S35); 124 kg of media (S36); chlorine only after the filter.

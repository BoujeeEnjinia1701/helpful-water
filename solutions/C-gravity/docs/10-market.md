# Helpful Water C: household gravity filter market and viability

Solution C is the program's household point-of-use product (HWP-MKT-001, household segment). Its prototype parts cost USD 86.40, and over an assumed 30,000 L module life that is about USD 0.003 a litre and USD 9.44 per person per year for a household of five, before the chlorine of solution D. That places C1 between sealed household filters (about USD 60 to 67 retail) and chlorine tablets (about USD 0.02 a litre), with an open design that regional workshops can build. Prices checked 2026-11-11.

> **Release limitation.** No prototype is used to treat water for drinking until its pathogen performance, materials safety, integrity, source suitability and claims are validated by qualified laboratories (HWP-CLM-001).

## Viability metrics

- Cost per litre over life: USD 0.003 (prototype parts over 30,000 L, assumed); add D's chlorine where used.
- Cost per person per year: USD 9.44 (prototype parts over 1.8 years for five people); the shortlist target of USD 10 per user per year (HWP-MKT-001) is met on paper.
- Sustained correct use at 3, 6 and 12 months: threshold to set with the partner (HWP-PLN-001 KPI).

## Open route

Drawings and the build plan under CERN-OHL-P-2.0 let regional workshops build the stand and print the small parts; buckets, taps and fasteners are bought locally; the module is bought from a qualified maker with data sheets and lot traceability. Helpful Engineering's volunteer network and WASH partners carry C to pilots once accredited tests are done.

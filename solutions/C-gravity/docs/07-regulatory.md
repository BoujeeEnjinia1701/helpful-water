# Helpful Water C: household gravity filter regulatory and standards register

Solution C carries the program register HWP-REG-001 and adds what is specific to a household gravity unit: food-contact containers, a timber stand, the WHO household scheme as the main procurement benchmark, and biocide rules for silver if option C2 were chosen. Nothing here is legal advice; anything marked "to assess" needs a qualified regulatory adviser before any product claim. Checked 2026-11-11.

> **Release limitation.** No prototype is used to treat water for drinking until its pathogen performance, materials safety, integrity, source suitability and claims are validated by qualified laboratories (HWP-CLM-001).

Table 1. Regulatory and standards register for C.

| # | Standard or regulation | Jurisdiction | Applicability to C | Owner (role) | Status |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| W1 | WHO International Scheme to Evaluate Household Water Treatment Technologies (HWP-REG-001 W1) | International | The main humanitarian benchmark for a household filter; C1 alone targets the bacteria and protozoa levels, C1 with D targets the three-star levels (4, 5, 4 log) | Program lead | Applies as a target; untested (HWP-CLM-001) |
| W2 | WHO Guidelines for drinking-water quality (W2) | International | E. coli verification point; chlorine 5 mg/L ceiling for D in the raw bucket | Microbiology lead | Applies |
| W3, W4 | EPA purifier guide standard; NSF P231 (W3, W4) | United States (reference) | Only for the combined C plus D system | Regulatory specialist | Not claimed at TRL 3 |
| W5 | NSF/ANSI 42 (W5) | United States (voluntary) | C has no carbon stage, so no aesthetic claim | Regulatory specialist | Does not apply |
| W6 | NSF/ANSI 53 and 401 (W6) | United States (voluntary) | No chemical claim | Regulatory specialist | Does not apply |
| W7 | NSF/ANSI/CAN 61 and 372 (W7) | United States and Canada (voluntary) | Every wetted part: buckets, lids, bulkhead, valve, hose, module, tee, check valve, tap, printed fittings | Materials specialist | Design rule (PR-016) |
| W9 | Pesticide or biocide registration (W9) | United States, European Union, others | C1: none (no silver or antimicrobial media). C2: silver-impregnated candles would need it before any antimicrobial claim | Regulatory specialist | Does not apply to C1; to assess for C2 |
| W10 | EU Drinking Water Directive (EU) 2020/2184, contact materials (W10) | European Union | Materials, if sold in the EU | Materials specialist | To assess |
| W11 | National registration in target countries (W11) | Partner countries | Before any distribution | Partner organisation | To assess per country |
| W12 | ISO 7010 and ISO 3864 (W12) | International | Pictograms on the raw and clean labels and the valve tag | Industrial designer | Applies as guidance |
| C1 | Food-contact status of the bought buckets (for example the US FDA food-contact rules for polyethylene) | Country of purchase | New buckets with the supplier's food-contact statement; never reused chemical pails | Procurement | Design rule; certificates to collect (HWC-DEC-001 C4) |
| C2 | Timber treatment | Country of build | The stand is not wetted; untreated or food-safe oiled timber only, no copper, chromium or arsenic preservatives near water containers | Builder | Design rule |
| C3 | Furniture tip-over practice (child pull and climb loads) | Reference | The stand is a tall loaded object in a home; the wall strap (HWC-DEC-001 D4) follows that practice | Mechanical engineer | To assess (standard to select) |
| C4 | Infant-use claims (HWP-CLM-001) | All | No claim that C serves infants until tested at the infant-use volume and paired with D | Program lead | Applies |
